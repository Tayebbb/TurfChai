# Loyalty & Rewards

## Tiers

| Tier | Points needed | Booking discount | Perks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silver | 0 | 0% | — |
| Gold | 1,000 | 10% | Free 30-min extension when available |
| Platinum | 2,000 | 15% | Priority booking + free 30-min extension |

## Earning points

You earn points for: completing bookings, attending matches, writing reviews, completing your profile, joining open games, off-peak bookings, and monthly activity bonuses. Point batches can expire — check the rewards page for expiry dates.

## Redeeming points

Available rewards:

- **৳50 off** — 500 points (wallet credit)
- **৳150 off** — 1,000 points (wallet credit)
- **Free 1-hour slot** — 2,000 points
- **10% off next booking** — 2,500 points
- **Priority Booking Pass** — 3,000 points

Wallet credits are applied automatically at checkout.

## Reliability score

Solo players have a reliability score (0–100, starts at 100). Attending games keeps it high; no-shows reduce it. Open games can require a minimum reliability score (commonly 90) to join.
