# Frequently Asked Questions

## What payment methods are supported?

bKash, Nagad, debit/credit cards, and cash for walk-in bookings at the venue counter.

## What sports can I book?

Football, Cricket, Futsal, Badminton, Basketball and Volleyball, depending on the venue's pitches.

## What are open games?

Open games let solo players join a match organized by someone else. Each game shows the skill level, price per player, and remaining spots. Payment is per player. Some games require a minimum reliability score.

## What are LFG alerts?

"Looking for game" alerts notify you when an open game matching your preferred sport, area, days and time window appears.

## How do tournaments work?

Hosts create tournaments at a venue with a team capacity, entry fee and prize pool. Teams register and pay the entry fee (a deposit plus balance schedule may apply). Fixtures are published per round. Tournaments can be open to everyone or invite-only via an invite link.

## Can I change my booking time?

Reschedule availability depends on the venue. If rescheduling is not offered, cancel (per the venue's cancellation policy) and book the new slot.

## How do I contact support?

Use the in-app support chat or email support@turfchai.com. For venue-specific issues (facilities, on-site payments), contact the venue directly from its page.

## Is my personal data shared?

Your profile is only visible to you and, in limited form (name, avatar), to game organizers and venue staff you book with. TurfChai does not sell personal data.
