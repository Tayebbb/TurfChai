# Venue Owner Guide

## Listing a venue

1. Submit a turf request with venue details, trade license and NID documents.
2. TurfChai admins review requests (approve, reject, or request changes). Requests get a code like TR-1042.
3. Once approved, the venue goes live and appears in search.

## Managing pitches and pricing

- Add each pitch with its format (5/6/7/9/11-a-side), surface and lighting details.
- Set slot duration (30–120 min), buffer time (5/10/15 min) and peak/off-peak rates per sport.
- Block slots for maintenance from the calendar.

## Staff roles

Owner, Co-owner, Manager, Front desk, and Accountant. Each role has configurable permissions: bookings calendar, cash logging, shift open/close, promotions, refunds, pricing edits, and payout visibility.

## Shifts and cash

Front-desk staff open and close shifts with an opening float. Walk-in cash payments are logged against the shift; the app tracks expected drawer amount versus counted amount at close (balanced or discrepancy).

## Payouts

- Payouts are settled per period to your registered bank account.
- The platform fee is **6% of online (non-cash) revenue only** — cash collected at the venue is not charged.
- Net payout = gross − platform fee − refunds.
- Payout statuses: scheduled → in transit → settled (or failed).

## Promotions

Create discounts (percentage, flat, buy-X-get-Y, off-peak specials) with validity windows and conditions. Active promotions show a badge on your venue card.
