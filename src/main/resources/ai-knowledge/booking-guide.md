# Booking Guide

## How booking works

1. Search venues by area, sport, date and budget.
2. Pick a venue and view its available slots for your date.
3. Select a slot — it is **held for 5 minutes** while you complete checkout. If payment isn't completed in time, the slot is released back to availability.
4. Pay with bKash, Nagad, card, or choose pay-at-venue where offered.
5. You receive a booking code like **TC-48291**. Keep it for check-in.

## Deposits

Venues set one of three deposit policies:

- **Full payment only** — pay the entire amount at booking.
- **30% deposit** — pay 30% now, the rest at the venue.
- **50% deposit** — pay 50% now, the rest at the venue.

## Split payments

If the venue allows it, you can split the booking cost with your squad:

- The captain creates the booking and invites members.
- Each member pays their own share through the app.
- A payment deadline applies; unpaid shares can be settled by the captain or the booking may be released.
- The booking is "partially paid" until all shares are in.

## Booking statuses

pending → confirmed → paid / partially_paid → completed. Bookings can also be cancelled, refunded, or marked no_show.

## Peak vs off-peak pricing

Venues price slots by time window. Off-peak (typically morning to late afternoon) is cheaper; peak (evenings) costs more. Exact windows and rates are set per venue and sport.
